Habit tracker questions

What is a habit tracker?

A habit tracker records whether you did a habit each day, so you can see your streak, your consistency and the days you tend to miss.

How long does it take to build a habit?

Often longer than 21 days. A well-known study by Lally and colleagues (2009) found it took 66 days on average, with a wide range from 18 to 254 days. That is why Grit offers 21, 41, 90, 180 and 365 day challenges.

What is the grit score?

The grit score runs from 0 to 1000. It rewards days kept, finished challenges, your longest streak and daily notes, scaled by how consistent and recent your effort is.

Is this habit tracker free?

Yes. Habit Tracker is free to use.

Is my data private?

Your challenges and notes belong to your account and are visible only to you. Passwords are hashed, you can export your data at any time, and you can permanently delete your account and everything in it from the Account page.

What happens if I miss a day?

The missed day shows on your calendar, your consistency drops a little and your streak resets. You can still tick the day later and carry on.
